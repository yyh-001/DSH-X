本预发布版本把「悬浮窗」改成系统级悬浮球，并收掉 WebView 里内嵌的悬浮胶囊。

### 新增功能

- **系统悬浮窗**：设置页「悬浮窗」默认关闭，打开时申请「显示在其他应用上层」权限；授权后显示 44dp 的小悬浮球，切到后台或回到桌面都还在，可拖动并记住位置，点按回到 DSH-X，长按直接关闭。

### 体验优化

- **WebView 回归全屏**：移除内嵌的实例胶囊和底部实例切换抽屉，页面完全全屏；返回控制台用系统返回手势或错误页按钮，切换实例回到控制台完成。

### 其他变更

- 核心包 `dsh-x-core` 升至 `0.2.1-rc.5`（桌面 Electron 外壳保持 `0.2.0`），重新打包并签名的 Android APK 随本版本发布。

Full Changelog: [v0.2.1-rc.4...v0.2.1-rc.5](https://github.com/yyh-001/DSH-X/compare/v0.2.1-rc.4...v0.2.1-rc.5)

## English

This pre-release turns the floating window into a system-level floating ball and removes the in-page floating pill from the WebView.

### New Features

- **System floating window**: The **Floating window** switch is off by default; enabling it asks for the "Display over other apps" permission, then shows a small 44dp floating ball that stays visible when the app is in the background or you are on the home screen. Drag to move (position is remembered), tap to return to DSH-X, long-press to close it.

### Improvements

- **Full-screen WebView**: Remove the in-page instance pill and the instance switcher sheet so pages use the whole screen; go back to the console with the system back gesture or the error-page button and switch instances from the console.

### Chores

- Bump `dsh-x-core` to `0.2.1-rc.5` (the Electron desktop shell stays at `0.2.0`); ship a rebuilt, signed Android APK with this release.
